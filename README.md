# Cross-Channel Temporal Fusion for Retail Customer Lifetime Value Prediction

## Published Research

**Journal:** Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence  
**Section:** AI in Business  
**Publication Date:** November 10, 2026  
**Volume:** 9  
**DOI:** 10.3389/frai.2026.1956496

## Research Overview

This research investigates artificial intelligence and
deep learning approaches for predicting retail customer
lifetime value (CLV).

The study examines a gated Bidirectional Gated Recurrent
Unit (BiGRU) fusion architecture for integrating
cross-channel customer information.
